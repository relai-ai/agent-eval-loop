# Code of conduct

We want agent-eval-loop to be a welcoming place to learn, report problems, and contribute.
Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to follow this code, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or whether they contribute code.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and make room for different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and work. Disagree without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, consent, and boundaries, including requests to stop an interaction.
- Acknowledge mistakes, accept reasonable correction, and help repair harm.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, insults directed at a person,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.
Repeatedly disrupting discussions or retaliating against someone for reporting a concern is
also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed
spaces, as well as public interactions when someone is representing the project. Maintainers
and contributors are held to the same standards.

## Report a concern

Contact [priyatham@relai.ai](mailto:priyatham@relai.ai) privately with a description of what
happened and any relevant links or context. Include only information you are comfortable
sharing and say if there are immediate safety concerns. Do not post private incident details
in an issue.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Information will be shared only as needed to assess the
report and respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Someone involved in a
report should not decide its outcome, and reports concerning a maintainer should be handled
by an uninvolved maintainer where available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, ask someone to change their behavior, restrict
participation temporarily, or ban a participant. The response should reflect the severity,
impact, and pattern of behavior. When appropriate, maintainers will explain the decision
privately and give the affected person an opportunity to respond.

To request reconsideration, reply through the private reporting channel with relevant new
context. An uninvolved maintainer should review the request where available. Good-faith
reports and requests for reconsideration must not result in retaliation.
