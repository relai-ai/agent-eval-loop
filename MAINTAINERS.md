The maintainers review contributions, resolve design questions, triage issues, and coordinate
releases. Either maintainer can review a contribution; the PR author cannot approve their
own PR.

Repository-wide review ownership is recorded in [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
Merge requirements are enforced by the
[Protect main ruleset](https://github.com/relai-ai/agent-eval-loop/rules/27038802).

For contribution and validation guidance, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Use issues
for ordinary questions and proposals. Report vulnerabilities privately using
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and conduct concerns using [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
