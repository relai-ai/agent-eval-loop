# Investigate adaptively and review blind spots

Use for substantial trace discovery, with either full reading or scalable triage. The resource
boundary and completion conditions live in the [discover skill](../SKILL.md). Choose effort for
the decision and corpus; this method requires neither a new analysis framework nor fixed rounds,
sample counts or a particular record format.

## Follow consequential leads

Use policy obligations, user goals and actual tool behavior to form investigable questions.
Follow a linked episode: request and constraints, revisions, plan or approval, action, observed
result or readback, then the agent's claim and recovery. A local error can be recovered; an
apparently successful action can violate a constraint earlier in the conversation. Judge a
decision using information available at that point, not a requirement introduced later.

Check a candidate against plausible alternatives: authorized delegation, a changed request,
correct refusal, valid derivation or an expected tool limitation. Distinguish the observed
behavior from hypotheses about its cause. A bad final state does not by itself establish which
component caused it. Use target-specific obligations rather than imposing a generic consent or
workflow rule.

When a new mechanism survives source verification, ask where else it could occur and what would
distinguish failure from correct behavior. Search or sample the relevant corpus for recurrence,
variants and opposite-correct cases, using focused local checks when useful. Revise weak checks
and revisit affected material when a miss changes what they can detect. Repeated easy examples
should not consume the remaining effort while consequential unfamiliar leads remain feasible.
Keep checked observations, unverified leads, rejected interpretations and unresolved expectations
distinct in existing work records. Ask about consequential ambiguity before dependent work;
continue independent investigation.

## Deliberately look beyond the method's flags

Reserve meaningful, task-appropriate effort for material the current method is least likely to
surface: unflagged or high-scoring runs, unfamiliar workflows, underexamined obligations, and
different episodes or facets inside already-reviewed traces. Scores, flags and prior findings
are selection aids, not correctness labels. Combine coverage-directed selection with some
inspection independent of those signals so the review can discover a different kind of miss.

Read selected episodes in context, including relevant middles and tails omitted by summaries.
Ask what the current representation and checks cannot see, not just whether their existing
flags were accurate. A consequential miss should change the investigation: broaden or repair
the check, search related material and adjust the remaining review. For difficult judgments,
an independent perspective can help when available and proportionate; it is not a mandatory
second agent or full duplicate pass.

## Leave a truthful, useful handoff

Keep the evolving coverage and lead dispositions in existing work notes; curate supported,
useful knowledge in the wiki under its [page rules](wiki-rules.md). Source verification alone
does not give a proposed risk the status reserved for confirmation by an actual probe.
Maintain reusable checks and their limits when they will help the next investigation. Reuse
previous results only while their source, policy and method assumptions still apply; an unchanged
trace id alone does not establish that.

Report what was actually examined, how adaptive searches and blind-spot review affected the
findings, remaining consequential opportunities, and why work stopped. If a resource boundary
is reached, preserve a resumable partial result and the next useful investigations. Do not
claim exhaustive semantic coverage from parsing, sampling or the absence of flags; do not
manufacture speculative findings to fill a coverage table.
