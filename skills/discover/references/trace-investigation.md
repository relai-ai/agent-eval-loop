# Investigate adaptively and review blind spots

Use for substantial trace discovery, with either full reading or scalable triage. The resource
boundary and coverage modes live in the [discover skill](../SKILL.md). Choose effort for the
request, corpus and existing knowledge; no fixed rounds, sample counts or tracking system are
required.

## Follow consequential leads

For broad discovery, compare what users wanted with what the agent chose, did and claimed across
meaningfully different cases. Include revised constraints, decision-relevant tool evidence,
effects and recovery. Compact episode views or grouped summaries can make this pass scalable;
validate them against full source and disclose omissions. Mechanical flags and terminal outcomes
do not substitute for these comparisons.

Use gaps in that view, policy obligations and actual tool behavior to form investigable questions.
Follow the relevant episode in context: a local error can be recovered, while an apparently
successful action can violate an earlier constraint. Judge a decision using the information
available then, rather than a requirement introduced later.

Check plausible alternatives such as a changed request, correct refusal, valid derivation or
expected tool limitation. Distinguish observed behavior from hypotheses about its cause; a bad
final state does not establish which component caused it. Verify the source authority for the
assumptions and relationships that make a suspected discrepancy a failure or justify a grading
rule. When that basis is missing, retain a qualified lead or resolve the question before dependent
work. Use the target's actual obligations rather than a generic workflow rule.

When a mechanism survives source verification, identify the expectation or relationship that
failed, then ask which other cases could violate it while passing the current checks. Choose
views and checks that preserve the facts needed to distinguish plausible answers; retrieve fuller
context or repair the view when it omits those facts. Where feasible, check the affected cases
rather than only collecting more examples of the original symptom. Verify candidates, meaningful
variants and opposite-correct cases against source, using focused local checks when useful.
Compare this deepening with unexplored questions and unchecked facets of familiar episodes;
more easy examples should not displace consequential unfamiliar leads. Keep checked observations,
rejected interpretations and unresolved expectations distinct; ask about consequential ambiguity
while continuing independent work.

## Deliberately look beyond the method's flags

Reserve meaningful, task-appropriate effort for material the method is least likely to surface:
unflagged or high-scoring runs, unfamiliar workflows, underexamined obligations and different
facets of already-reviewed traces. Scores, flags and prior findings guide selection; they do not
establish correctness. Combine coverage-directed selection with some inspection independent of
those signals so a different kind of miss can emerge.

Read selected episodes in context, including relevant middles and tails omitted by summaries.
Ask what the representation and checks cannot see, not just whether their existing flags were
accurate. When a consequential miss changes what the method can detect, broaden or repair useful
checks, revisit affected material and adjust the remaining investigation.

## Maintain useful knowledge

Keep useful coverage and lead dispositions in existing work notes; curate supported knowledge
under the [wiki rules](wiki-rules.md). Source verification alone does not give a proposed risk
the status reserved for confirmation by a probe. Maintain reusable checks and their limits when
they help future work. Reuse earlier results only while their source, policy and method assumptions
apply; an unchanged trace ID alone does not establish that. Link canonical records rather than
repeating the full analysis.

## Assess completion and hand off

Before stopping, judge whether feasible work within scope and resources could add distinct useful
understanding, resolve consequential uncertainty or change an evaluation decision. A stable
priority shortlist alone is insufficient. Pursue worthwhile remaining work within the agreement;
respect bounded requests without requiring exhaustive reading or spending the full allowance.

Report what was actually examined, how follow-up and blind-spot checks changed the findings,
remaining opportunities and why work stopped. If the resource boundary prevents worthwhile
in-scope work, leave a resumable partial result. Keep uncertainty and coverage gaps visible;
processing, sampling or absence of flags does not establish exhaustive understanding. Do not
manufacture findings or a new ledger to justify completion.
